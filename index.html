Egor Media Studio

An internal publishing tool for a small network of educational YouTube channels, including DEPTHWORK.

What it does

  • Applies channel branding prepared by the channel owner: description, keywords, banner, watermark and playlists.
  • Uploads finished videos privately, with thumbnails, chapters, captions and audience settings.
  • Schedules publication only for videos the owner has approved.
  • Reads channel analytics so the owner can see what works.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the channels and the people they authorize. It is not a public service and does not accept new users.
