Privacy Policy

Egor Media Studio · effective 2 October 2026 · updated 2 October 2026

Who we are

Egor Media Studio is an internal tool used by the owner of a small network of YouTube channels to manage those channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the tool uses the YouTube API Services to access, for that channel only:

  • channel settings and branding (description, keywords, banner, watermark, playlists);
  • the channel's videos, their metadata, thumbnails and captions;
  • comments, for moderation by the owner;
  • channel analytics and, where available, revenue reports.

The tool does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube Analytics provides to the channel owner.

How the data is used

Only to perform the actions the channel owner requests: apply branding, upload and schedule the owner's own videos, and show the owner their channel statistics. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Google access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the macOS Keychain. Channel statistics may be cached on the same computer to build reports. No data is stored on third-party servers.

Cookies and device information

The tool runs only on the owner's own computer. It does not place cookies, does not use tracking or analytics technologies and does not collect information from users' devices. This website does not use cookies or analytics either.

Sharing

Information obtained through YouTube API Services is not shared with any third party, except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Cached analytics are kept while the channel is managed with the tool and deleted on request. Access can be revoked at any time, which also deletes the stored token:

  • in your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions, remove “Egor Media Studio”;
  • or by the owner running the tool's revoke command.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Egor Media Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to delete data: open an issue or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of Egor Media Studio.
